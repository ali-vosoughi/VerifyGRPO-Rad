# OpenRadAgent

Open agentic radiology: multi-agent planning and generation of radiology reports with open-weight vision-language models, report verification, and reinforcement-learning post-training.

This repository will hold the code, evaluation harness and model recipes of the lane as they are released, together with the papers that come out of it. Nothing here makes a claim ahead of the papers.

## Lineage

- Multi-agent planning and generation of radiology reports using multimodal agentic frameworks. SPIE Emerging Topics in Artificial Intelligence (ETAI) 2026. [Paper page](https://ali-vosoughi.github.io/publications/radagent/)
- Follow-on work on open-weight agents, verification and RL post-training: in preparation.

## Status

- Code: being prepared for release; the first drop is the evaluation harness.
- Models and data: open-weight models only; datasets under their own licences (CheXpert Plus, CheXlocalize, Open-i), not redistributed here.
